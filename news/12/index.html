IceCast Streamer is completed, up and running.

November 3, 2024

I finally completed IceCast Streamer and launched it!

In order to launch an Internet Radio, the typical solution would be to use Icecast + Liquidsoap. Icecast is used for delivering the audio stream to listeners, while Liquidsoap would do all the playlist management, crossfades and music re-encoding.

Luckily, now you don't need to use Liquidsoap anymore, you can use IceCast Streamer instead! It is free and open source, it is written in C++ and lightweight. Enjoy using it!

Test IceCast Streamer in your browser!
