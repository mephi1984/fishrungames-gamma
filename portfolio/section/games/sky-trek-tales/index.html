Sky Trek Tales

Go to the main Sky Trek Tales website

Sky Trek Tales is an third-person online multiplayer space simulator. Explore endless space, collect flying containers, shoot other players!

The game is free to play. No download required. You can play from your PC or any mobile device. Just open the browser and play.

Play Online

Screenshots

The game was developed for Web using C++, OpenGL 3.0, SDL2 and Emscripten. The multiplayer is implemented using WebSockets.
